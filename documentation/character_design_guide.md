# CODEQUEST — Character Design & Aesthetic Guide

## 1. Visual Direction & Style

CODEQUEST blends a **stylized cinematic animated aesthetic** (inspired by modern character-driven sci-fi animation like *Spider-Verse*, *Arcane*, and *Overwatch*) with high-clarity educational readability.

### Core Visual Tenets:
1. **Readable Silhouette**: Clean forms distinguishable at both mobile zoom and full-screen desktop cinematic framing.
2. **Expressive Proportions**: Friendly, stylized head-to-body ratio (~1:6.5 for ARIA) with large expressive eyes and distinct facial structures.
3. **Controlled Holographic Language**: ARIA is a synthetic, living hologram. Rather than blinding white or flat transparent blue, she features delicate cyan highlights, deep indigo-blue structural garments, and soft violet chromatic accents.
4. **Physicality in Sci-Fi**: Hard-surface cybernetic paneling is paired with soft organic forms so characters feel solid, expressive, and relatable.

## 2. Character Specification: ARIA

- **Designation**: Advanced Responsive Intelligence Assistant
- **Creator**: Dr. Mira Nair (Aethelgard Deep Cybernetics)
- **Role**: Co-pilot, emotional compass, and programming mentor.
- **Palette**:
  - Primary Base: `#1E1B4B` (Deep Abyssal Indigo)
  - Holographic Glow: `#38BDF8` (Bright Luminescent Cyan, 450nm)
  - Secondary Accent: `#818CF8` (Soft Periwinkle Violet)
  - Highlight Specular: `#F8FAFC` (Pure Pearl White)
- **Facial Aesthetics**:
  - Expressive anime/pixar-style oval eyes with emissive iris rings.
  - Delicate, stylized nose and defined mouth for readable speech animation.
  - Floating holographic hair locks that pulse gently with speech cadence.
