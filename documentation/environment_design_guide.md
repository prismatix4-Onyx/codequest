# CODEQUEST — Environment Design Guide

## 1. Setting: The Spaceship ORPHEUS

The ORPHEUS is an abandoned exploration and training vessel adrift in the outer orbit of Kepler-186f. It was once the premier research outpost of the Aethelgard Initiative before the sudden shutdown.

### Key Visual Zones of the Bridge:
1. **The Panoramic Observation Canopy**:
   - Sweeping curved armored glass facing deep space and the distant planetary nebula.
   - Framing elements: Structural bulkheads with recessed emergency conduits.
2. **Central Reactor Platform**:
   - Elevated hexagonal dais housing the **Core Confinement Chamber**.
   - Dual circulating energy rings and primary magnetic stabilizer pillars.
3. **Pilot & Navigation Stations**:
   - Angled flight consoles with tactile physical levers and interactive holographic monitors.
4. **Hologram Emitting Dais**:
   - Flush circular floor projector from which ARIA materializes.

---

## 2. Dynamic Lighting States

The ORPHEUS environment uses 4 distinct lighting profiles that reflect story progress:

1. **State 0: Low-Power Standby (Default Opening)**
   - Ambient deep blue/navy tint (`#0B0F19`).
   - Sparse floor path lights and dim console backlighting.
2. **State 1: Critical Emergency (Reactor Failure)**
   - High-contrast pulsing emergency red beacons (`#EF4444`).
   - Shadows deepen, alarm strobes cycle at 1.2 Hz.
3. **State 2: Diagnostic & Stabilization (Coding Challenge)**
   - Focused amber/cyan task lighting around player consoles and code projection.
4. **State 3: Reactor Online / Operational (Mission Victory)**
   - Warm golden-cyan primary illumination, crystalline core glow, activated particle conduits.
